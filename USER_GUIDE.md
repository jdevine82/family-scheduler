# Devine Family Planner: user guide

## The calendar

- **Day** shows a lane per person. Things for **Everyone** stretch across all lanes. **Week** shows people as rows and days as columns. **Month** is the big picture: dots are weekly routines.
- **Fixed events** (dentist, swimming) count as done once their time has passed, and fade.
- **Flexible jobs** (dashed border) are plans. Once their time passes they get an amber dot until someone checks in.
- ↻ means it repeats every week. **G** means it comes from a Google calendar (read-only). 💰 means it's a paid pocket money job.

## Adding and changing things

There are no logins. On the wall, a computer or a tablet, tap **✏️ Edit** first (it switches off after 2 minutes without a touch, so stray taps from the kids don't move things). On a phone you can edit straight away.


- Tap an empty spot, or the **+** button on phones, to add something. Tick **Repeats every week** for routines, and choose the days.
- Tap an item to see it, then **Edit** or **Delete**. For a routine, you're asked whether you mean just this one, this and future weeks, or every week.
- On a computer, **drag** items to move them, including into another person's lane, and drag the bottom edge to change the length. On a phone or touchscreen, **press and hold**, then drag.

## Tasks

- Folders of to-dos live in the left sidebar (computer), the **Tasks** tab (phone), or behind **☑ Tasks** on the wall.
- Drag a task onto someone's lane, or tap **Schedule**. Drag a scheduled item back onto the task list to unschedule it.
- Tick the circle when it's done. Badges show **Needs check-in**, **Underway** and **Pushed back N×**.

## Daily check-in

Tap **Check-in**, tap your name, and answer each past flexible job:

- **Done**: it's ticked off.
- **Underway**: pick when you'll carry on, or send it back to the task list.
- **Needs rescheduling**: it goes back to the task list, or you can pick a new time now. For routines, move this one or skip this week.
- **Ask me later**: it comes up again next time.

## The wall display

- Tap anything for details. Swipe or use ‹ › to change day. It goes back to today after 2 minutes without a touch, can switch views by itself, and dims at night.
- When something starts, it plays that person's chime and shows a card. Reminders play a softer chime.
- **✏️ Edit** lets you change the calendar from the wall. Tap empty space to add, tap an item to change it, and hold and drag to move it. Tap **Done** when you've finished. It also turns off by itself after 2 minutes without a touch.
- **⚙** opens Settings, which needs the parent PIN.
- **🔊 / 🔇** mutes or unmutes this screen's chimes (sound is on by default). **🌙** means it's quiet hours (8:30 pm – 7 am by default, set in Settings → Display & chimes): alerts still pop up but don't chime. An amber dot on the speaker means the browser is waiting for one touch before it will play sound.

## Weekly report

Tap a person's **name** at the top of the day view (or their row in week view). On a phone, use **More → 📊 Weekly reports**. It shows their last 7 days: every item marked ✓ done, ✗ not done, 🔧 underway, ⏳ still to come or ↩ moved, with daily and weekly totals. It's on screen only, and it goes back to the calendar after 2 minutes without a touch.

## Pocket money (💰 Money tab)

- Kids can see their balance, **History**, and the **Jobs you can do** list without a PIN.
- **Add to my schedule**: pick who, then a day. Only free times are offered, so it never clashes with anything else in that child's lane.
- When the time comes, the job shows under **Waiting for a parent**. **Approve & pay** (PIN) adds the money. **Not done** pays nothing.
- **Fine**, **Withdraw** and **Money setup** (weekly money, payday, the jobs list, change PIN) all need the parent PIN. It stays unlocked for 5 minutes, or until you tap **Lock** or leave the tab.
- Weekly money is paid automatically on payday.
